✅ PART 2 · MIXED PRACTICE 20 min

Five mixed reps, drawing on the whole week — declining, parsing, identifying, and translating in one place. Then build a sentence of your own. Wrong answers give a hint; use Reset to try again.

✅ TRY IT · decline
Make dominus the accusative plural (“the masters,” as object). Which form?
✅ TRY IT · parse
In Puerī templum spectant (“the boys gaze at the temple”), what case and number is puerī?
✅ TRY IT · translate
Translate: Puer amīcum videt. (videt = “sees”)
✅ TRY IT · the neuter rule
What is the nominative plural of the neuter noun oppidum (town)?
✅ TRY IT · identify
The dictionary lists templum, templī. Which declension and gender is it?

Write your own: make one full Latin sentence meaning “The master calls the boys.” Use a nominative singular subject and an accusative plural object (the verb “calls” is vocat). Then reveal one correct version.

Dominus puerōs vocat. — dominus (nom. sg. subject) + puerōs (acc. pl. object of puer) + vocat (“calls”). If you wrote puerī for the object, check the case: an object needs the accusative, and the accusative plural of puer is puerōs.
