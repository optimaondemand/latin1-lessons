★ Where the Beat Falls — Syllables & the Penult Rule
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
In English, stress is a guessing game. In Latin, one clean rule tells you exactly where the emphasis goes — every time.
Why is it PHO-tograph but pho-TO-grapher? English never really tells you — you just have to know. Latin is kinder: once you can break a word into syllables, a single rule about the second-to-last syllable (the penult) decides the stress with no exceptions. Learn it today and you can read any Latin word aloud with confidence, including every name in the Aeneas story.

📚 PART 2 · COUNTING SYLLABLES 8 min

Step one: break it apart

A Latin word has as many syllables as it has vowels and diphthongs. (Remember: a diphthong like ae or au counts as one.) A few simple habits tell you where to make the cut:

  • A single consonant between two vowels goes with the following vowel: Rō-ma, a-qua.
  • When two consonants come together, they usually split, one to each side: puel-la, mag-nus.
  • A diphthong stays whole as one syllable: nau-ta, Cae-sar.

📌 PART 3 · THE PENULT RULE 12 min

Step two: find the beat

Latin has names for the last three syllables: the ultima (last), the penult (second-to-last), and the antepenult (third-to-last). The stress falls by these rules:

If the word has…Stress falls on…
two syllablesthe first syllable
three or more, and the penult is longthe penult
three or more, and the penult is shortthe antepenult

A penult counts as long if it has a long vowel (a macron), a diphthong, or is followed by two consonants. Otherwise it's short. And one bonus rule: Latin never stresses the very last syllable of a word of two or more.

Take the name of the peninsula Aeneas is fated to reach: Ītalia. Divide it into syllables, decide where the stress goes, and explain why. Then reveal.

Ī-ta-li-a — four syllables. The penult is li, which is short (short vowel, single consonant), so the stress backs up to the antepenult: Ī-TA-li-a (“ee-TAH-lee-ah”). Note that even though the first vowel Ī is long, length only decides the stress when it's on the penult.
