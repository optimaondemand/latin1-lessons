✅ TRY IT · spot the family
Which of these infinitives is third conjugation?

Write your own: make one full Latin sentence meaning “The Romans send a letter.” Use a nominative plural subject and the right verb form (epistula = letter, acc. sg. epistulam). Then reveal one correct version.

Rōmānī epistulam mittunt. — Rōmānī (nom. pl. subject) + epistulam (acc. sg. object) + mittunt (3rd pl. of mittere). If you wrote mittint, remember the third-plural vowel is u, not i: mittunt.
📌 PART 3 · THE THREE FAMILIES AT A GLANCE 12 min

Keep them straight for the quiz

The most common mistake on the graded work is mixing up the families — especially the 2nd and 3rd, whose infinitives look almost the same. Study this once more before you go:

FamilyInfinitive“they” formTell
1stportāreportantlong ā; they-form -ant
2ndvidērevidentlong ē; they-form -ent
3rddūceredūcuntshort e; they-form -unt

A quick trick for the “they” form: -ant (1st), -ent (2nd), -unt (3rd). If you see -unt, it's a third-conjugation verb — that u belongs to no one else.
