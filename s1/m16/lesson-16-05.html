✅ PART 2 · MIXED PRACTICE 20 min

Five mixed reps, drawing on the whole week — forming imperatives and vocatives and building a “don't.” Then compose a full command of your own.

✅ TRY IT · the rebel singular
Order one person to lead, using dūcere. Which form?
✅ TRY IT · address by name
You call out to Mārcus. Which form?
✅ TRY IT · say “don't” to a group
Tell a group “do not fight!”
✅ TRY IT · command a group
Tell a group of visitors to look, using spectāre. Which form?
✅ TRY IT · the -ius vocative
How do you address your son (fīlius)?

Write your own museum-entrance line: welcome a group of visitors by name, command them (as a group) to enter and look, and tell them not to touch. Then reveal one correct version.

Salvēte, hospitēs! Intrāte et spectāte! Nōlīte tangere! — “Greetings, guests! Enter and look! Do not touch!” Hospitēs (voc. pl. = nom. pl.), plural imperatives intrāte and spectāte, and the plural negative command nōlīte tangere. Speaking to one visitor instead? Salvē, hospes! Intrā et spectā! Nōlī tangere!
